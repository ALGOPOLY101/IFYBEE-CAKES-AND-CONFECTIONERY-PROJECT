WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Structured for real-world skills.

Our syllabus balances clear instruction with hands-on application, ensuring every student leaves with genuine kitchen confidence.

01

Cake Science & Sponges

Master recipes for vanilla sponge, moist chocolate, and velvety red velvet. Understand ingredient functions, leaveners, and oven temperature control.

02

Buttercream & Sharp Edges

Whip silky American and Swiss meringue buttercreams. Learn leveling, crumb coating, smooth acrylic disk finishes, and textured comb techniques.

03

Fondant & Sugar Art

Roll, drape, and smooth fondant without tears. Create delicate sugar blossoms, borders, bows, and silicone mold design accents.

04

Commercial Pastry & Costing

Bake flaky golden meat pies, roll sausage snacks, and fry glazed doughnuts. Learn recipe costing, cake pricing formulas, and bakery management basics.
